1. Introduction
House Rules ("we," "our," or "us") is an independently developed mobile application ("App") available on iOS. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use House Rules.
By using House Rules, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App.

2. Information We Collect
a. Information You Provide

Sign In with Apple: When you create an account using Sign In with Apple, we receive your Apple-assigned user identifier and, if you choose to share it, your name and email address. Apple may provide a private relay email address instead of your real email.
Date of Birth: We collect your date of birth to verify that you are 18 years of age or older before granting access to alcohol-related content within the App.

b. Information Generated Through Your Use of the App

Game Activity: We collect data related to your in-app activity, including games you mark as favorites, ratings you submit, comments you post, and play session data.
User-Generated Content: Any content you submit to the App, such as comments or ratings, is stored and associated with your account.

c. Device and Technical Information

We may collect standard device and usage data such as device type, operating system version, app version, crash logs, and general performance diagnostics to operate and improve the App.


3. Age Restrictions and Minor Protection
House Rules is intended for users 18 years of age or older. We require date-of-birth verification at sign-up and do not permit accounts for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we discover an account belongs to a user under 18, the account and associated data will be deleted.

4. How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect to:

Create and manage your account
Authenticate your identity via Sign In with Apple
Verify your age before granting access to age-restricted content
Save your preferences, favorites, ratings, and comments
Track play sessions for app functionality and personalization
Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
Improve, maintain, and secure the App
Comply with legal obligations


5. Advertising
The App does not currently display advertisements. We intend to introduce advertising features in a future update. When advertising is introduced, we will update this Privacy Policy and provide appropriate notice. At that time, advertising partners may collect certain identifiers and usage data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

While we take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.
For Supabase's data practices, see: https://supabase.com/privacy
